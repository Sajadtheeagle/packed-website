How to Get More HVAC Leads in Ottawa (2026)

Seven channels that actually fill an HVAC schedule — ranked by cost, speed, and who owns the lead.

Get My Free Audit

Every HVAC company in Ottawa wants the same thing: a phone that rings with real jobs, especially in the shoulder seasons when the heat waves and cold snaps quiet down. Here's how to get more HVAC leads in Ottawa in 2026 — the channels that work, ranked, with the trade-offs nobody tells you.

1. Win the Google Maps top 3 (highest ROI, slower)

When a furnace dies, homeowners search “HVAC near me” and call from the map — roughly 70% of those calls go to the top three results. Getting there is about your Google Business Profile: the right primary category (“HVAC contractor”), complete services, real job photos, and a steady stream of reviews. It takes 60–90 days to climb, but once you're there it produces calls month after month with no per-lead fee.

2. Local Services Ads (fastest paid calls)

Google's pay-per-lead ads (the “Google Guaranteed” / Verified ones at the very top) put you in front of emergency searchers this week. HVAC leads run roughly $60–$120 each, you only pay for real leads, and you can dispute junk — most contractors don't, and leave money on the table. A verified Google Business Profile is now required to run them.

3. Reviews (the cheapest lead multiplier)

Two HVAC companies, same distance: the one with 300 fresh reviews beats the one with 30, every time. Ask at the moment of thanks, text the direct review link within 10 minutes, and aim for 2–3 fresh reviews a month. It's free and it lifts every other channel.

4. A website that converts (stop the leak)

About 90% of contractor websites fail to turn a visit into a call — no click-to-call, no quote form, slow on mobile. Fixing that doesn't get you more traffic; it gets you more jobs from the traffic you already have, which is cheaper than buying more.

5. Missed-call text-back (recover what you're losing)

You're on a roof or under a furnace; the call goes to voicemail; 85% of those callers never leave a message — they call the next company. An automatic “Sorry we missed you, what's the job?” text catches them. The cheapest HVAC lead is the one already in your missed-call log.

6. Database reactivation (your past customers)

Before a shoulder season, a simple text/email campaign to your past customers (“time for your pre-winter tune-up?”) books jobs at a fraction of the cost of buying new leads. You already earned their trust; remind them you exist.

7. Search ads (scale, once the rest converts)

Google Search ads on high-intent keywords work — about $50–$60 per call when tightly managed — but only point them at a site and follow-up that convert. Ads aimed at a leaky bucket just drain your budget faster.

The order that works

Fix follow-up first (text-back, reviews), then own the map pack, then layer paid ads. That sequence plugs the leaks before you pour in more water. If you'd like a free read on where your HVAC business stands across all seven, our free audit covers exactly this.

What's the fastest way to get HVAC leads in Ottawa?

Local Services Ads and missed-call text-back work within days. Google Maps rankings take 60–90 days but become your cheapest long-term source.

How much do HVAC leads cost in Ottawa?

Roughly $60–$120 on Local Services Ads, ~$50–$60 per call on managed search ads, and far less per won job once you rank in the Google map pack.
