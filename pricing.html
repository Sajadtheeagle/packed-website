Common questions

I just want a website. What do I pay?

$2,950 for the website on its own — built, yours to keep, no monthly fee. Or $3,950 for the Launch Bundle, which adds your Google Business Profile setup and a brand refresh (a better deal if you need all three). No monthly commitment required for either.

Do I have to sign up for a monthly plan?

No. Buy any single service — a website, a logo, just local SEO — on its own. The monthly plans simply bundle several services for less and manage them as one system. Many contractors start with the $749 Door-Opener to see results first.

Can I mix and match?

Yes. Start with a website, add missed-call text-back next month, layer in SEO when you're ready. We'll always tell you the single most valuable next step — the free audit usually makes it obvious.

What's NOT included in the prices?

Ad spend (paid directly to Google/Meta — your account, your money, full visibility) and third-party costs like premium stock or print runs. No markups, no surprises.

Why are you cheaper than the big agencies?

Lower overhead, no account-manager layers, and entry pricing while we build our Ottawa case-study wall. Prices rise as proof accumulates — locking in now is the best deal we'll ever offer.
