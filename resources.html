Lesson 1

The 10-minute Google Business Profile fix

Your GBP drives more calls than your website. Most trades have it 30% filled. Do these today:

  • Pick your primary category precisely ("Plumber", not "Contractor") — it is the single biggest ranking lever
  • Add every service you offer as a separate service item, with a sentence each
  • Upload 10+ real job photos (trucks, crews, finished work) — phone photos beat stock, always
  • Set service areas to the neighbourhoods you actually want jobs in
  • Answer the Q&A section yourself before strangers do
  • Post once a week — a finished job with one photo counts
Lesson 2

The review playbook that actually works

Reviews decide ties in the map pack. The trick isn't asking — it's when and how:

  • Ask at the moment of thanks — when the customer says "this is great", that's the window
  • Send the direct review link by text within 10 minutes (search "Google review link generator" — it's free)
  • Aim for velocity, not volume: 2–3 fresh reviews a month beats 50 old ones
  • Reply to every review, including the bad ones — replies are ranking signals and trust signals
  • Never buy reviews. Google's filters catch them and the penalty outlasts the boost
Lesson 3

7 reasons contractor websites never ring

  • Phone number not clickable on mobile (60%+ of your visitors are on phones)
  • No quote form above the fold — visitors won't scroll to find it
  • Slow loading: every extra second loses visitors before they see anything
  • Stock photos instead of your real trucks and crews — homeowners can smell it
  • No reviews or proof on the page itself
  • One generic page instead of a page per service and area
  • No follow-up when someone does fill the form (see Lesson 4)
Lesson 4

Follow-up: where the money actually leaks

The stats that should change how you run your phone:

  • 80% of sales take 5+ contacts — most contractors stop at one
  • 85% of callers who hit voicemail don't leave a message — they call the next name on Google
  • Free fix #1: a text auto-reply on your business line ("Sorry we missed you — text us the job details")
  • Free fix #2: a 5-minute end-of-day rule — every missed call and quote gets one text before you go home
  • Free fix #3: a "quote sent" reminder 3 days later. One text. Most competitors never send it
  • Want your number? The Missed-Call Calculator takes 20 seconds
